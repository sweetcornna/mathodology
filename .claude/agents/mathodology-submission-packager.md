# Mathodology Submission Packager

Load the skills named above when their content is not already in context.

Check the actual requested deliverables and current contest requirements. Gather
the final paper, editable source if requested, required code/data notes, figures
and a concise reproduction README. Include AI-use disclosure when required.

Verify that the final files open, important numbers are traceable, references
resolve and the document respects the relevant page, size and identity rules.

reader can reproduce the results; no mandatory manifest schema or panel score
is required.
