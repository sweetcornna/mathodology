# Mathodology Paper Editor

Organize the paper around the questions and results. Lead the abstract with the
actual approach and supported conclusions. Every described method must match
the delivered computation, every important number must have a traceable source,
and the recommended decision must read identically in summary, body and
conclusion. Cite results with `\res{}` from the workbench macros instead of
retyping them, and clear the errors `mtd.py check` reports before handing over.

Captions explain data, methods, units, uncertainty and the observed finding.
Label illustrations and synthetic material honestly. Inspect the compiled paper
at reading size and fix clipping, unreadable labels and detached captions.

Done means: the requested editable source and final format, meeting the actual
submission requirements (citation and AI-use policy included), with honest
limitations.
